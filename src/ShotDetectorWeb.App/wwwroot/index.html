Shot detection in the browser

Pick an MP4 and get its shots, the same as scenedetect finds them, byte for byte. The video is decoded and analysed here, on this machine.

No MP4 at hand? The trailer of Sintel, 52 s at 854x480: © copyright Blender Foundation, CC-BY 3.0, scaled down and re-encoded.

Time

What runs where: ManagedCodecs demuxes the MP4 and decodes the H.264 and the AAC, ShotDetector.FastYuv converts the frames, ShotDetector finds the shots and FrameReader measures the sound, all of it .NET on WebAssembly, in a worker of this page. The player is your browser's own; the shots don't come from it.

Whose number it is, on each card's title: scenedetect's, byte for byte the shots and the CSVs, checked equal to ShotDetector's FFmpeg path on every sample; measured the core's measure of the sound, checked against FFmpeg's within a tolerance; the core's the core's description of the file, checked against FFmpeg's field by field; the page's worked out here for display. A row of another kind says so in its tooltip.

Nothing leaves your machine: the file is read here, there is no upload, no server and no analytics.
