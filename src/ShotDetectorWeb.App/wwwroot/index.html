Shot detection in the browser

Pick an MP4 and get its shots, the same as scenedetect finds them, byte for byte. The video is decoded and analysed here, on this machine.

Scenes CSV Stats CSV

ShotStartEndLength

What runs where: ManagedCodecs demuxes the MP4 and decodes the H.264, ShotDetector.FastYuv converts the frames and ShotDetector finds the shots, all of it .NET on WebAssembly, in a worker of this page. The player is your browser's own; the shots don't come from it.

Nothing leaves your machine: the file is read here, there is no upload, no server and no analytics.
