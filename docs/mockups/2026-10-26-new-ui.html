What runs where: ManagedCodecs demuxes the MP4 and decodes the H.264 and the AAC, ShotDetector.FastYuv converts the frames and ShotDetector finds the shots, all of it .NET on WebAssembly, in a worker of this page. The player is your browser's own; the shots don't come from it.

Marks: scenedetect's, byte for byte the CSVs, checked equal on every sample; measured the core's number, checked against FFmpeg within a tolerance; the page's worked out on the page for display.

Nothing leaves your machine: the file is read here, there is no upload, no server and no analytics. Source · MIT license · Third-party notices (ShotDetector.FastYuv is LGPL-2.1-or-later, its own replaceable file)
