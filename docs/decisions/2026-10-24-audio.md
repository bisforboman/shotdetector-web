# Audio: decoded in the worker by ManagedCodecs (2026-10-24)

The page shows a waveform and, after it, measures of the sound: a reference tone, silence, peaks, loudness. All of
them come from the audio track's samples, so they share one question: who decodes the audio? The rows are in
[../backlog.md](../backlog.md) ("A waveform from ManagedCodecs' AAC", "Audio measures"). The page's other decisions
are in [2026-10-24-the-page.md](2026-10-24-the-page.md).

## Who decodes the audio

### Question

Shot detection needs the video decoded by ManagedCodecs, because its results must equal FFmpeg's. Nothing on the
audio side has that requirement. The stills per shot come from the browser's own decode ("Pictures of shots"). Should
the audio come from the browser too?

### Choices

1. **The browser's Web Audio** (`decodeAudioData` on the file): a few lines in `app.js`, nothing added to the bundle.
   It decodes the whole track into memory at once, and each browser's decoder rounds a little differently.
2. **ManagedCodecs' `AacDecoder` in the worker** (AAC LC and HE-AAC, within 3e-7 of FFmpeg's float output): more code
   in the core and the App, the decoder in the bundle, and decode time added to every run. It decodes a packet at a
   time, gives the same samples in every browser, and keeps the showcase's story: every decoder on the page is
   managed code.

### Answer

**2**, the owner's choice: the story matters more than the code and the bundle's growth. How it is done:

- **One pass, never the whole track.** The core reads the audio packets in order, decodes each and folds its samples
  into what the rows need (the waveform's buckets, the measures' running state), then drops them. Memory stays
  small, whatever the file's length.
- **Its own export, after `Detect`.** Detection's time and results stay as they are. The waveform and the measures
  come from the same export, in the same pass.
- **Only AAC.** A track in another codec, or one `AacDecoder` refuses, gets one line on the page saying why there's
  no sound panel, not an error. A file without audio shows nothing.
- **Checked against FFmpeg where FFmpeg measures the same thing**, within a tolerance, not byte for byte: loudness
  against its `ebur128` filter, silence against `silencedetect`. FFmpeg has no reference-tone filter, but
  ShotDetector does (`FindReferenceTone`, on the FFmpeg path): the tone is found by its definition and checked
  against it. The decoded samples aren't bit-exact (3e-7), and the
  scene and stats CSVs stay the only outputs checked byte for byte. README "Checks" says so.
- **The cost is measured, not guessed**: the waveform row records how much the bundle grows and how long the decode
  takes on the Sintel trailer.

"Pictures of shots" is unchanged: a still is a picture of the video, and the browser's decode is enough for it.
